## The context

Clevermann helps Vietnamese candidates study, work and settle in Germany. Nobody buys that on impulse. People think about it for months, talk to family, compare options and come back many times before they sign.

So the ads were never the whole story. They were the first step of a long journey, and they had to fit the moment the person was in. I ran the Meta and Google accounts myself, with a monthly ad budget of more than VND 200M across channels.

## Push and pull are different traffic

The most expensive mistake in paid media is treating all traffic the same.

**Pull traffic** comes from people who are already looking. On Google Search and Google Maps, someone types "du học nghề Đức" or our brand name. The intent is there. The job is to match the question exactly, answer it fast and make the next step obvious.

**Push traffic** comes from people who were not looking. On Facebook and Instagram, the ad interrupts a scroll. Most of these people are early in their decision: they know they want a better future, not yet that Germany or Clevermann is the answer. Asking them to fill in a long form is asking too much, too soon.

## How a CRO foundation shaped the ads

I ran the ad accounts with the same lenses I used for 200+ A/B tests at audibene.

- **Relevance first.** One campaign per program and audience, so every ad and every message spoke to one situation, not to everyone.
- **The right first step.** On Meta, campaigns were optimised for a Messenger conversation, a small and easy yes, not for clicks. A conversation lets a counsellor meet the person where they are.
- **Awareness, not just targeting.** Push ads spoke to the problem and the possible future. On search, ads and pages answered concrete questions: cost, timeline, requirements.
- **Judge by what happens after.** A cheap click means nothing if it never becomes a consultation. Every campaign was read against the next steps in the funnel, not just its own dashboard. [Read the story.](/stories/the-lead-wasnt-the-finish-line/)

Push and pull also work together. People who first met Clevermann in a Facebook feed later searched for us by name: the brand is the top search term on our Google Business Profile.

## Results

| Channel | Role in the journey | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads (2025) | Pull: people already searching | **216,000 clicks** and **3.43M impressions** at **~VND 781 per click** (~VND 169M in total) |
| Meta Ads (Nov 2025 – Jan 2026) | Push: starting the conversation | **3,024 conversations** at **~USD 2.23 each**, **440,000 people** reached |
| Google Business Profile | Pull: brand and local search | Top search terms were **the Clevermann brand** and "du học nghề Đức" |

These ads fed the funnel described in the [Clevermann case study](/case-studies/clevermann/), where cost of sale fell from about 30% to below 10% of revenue.

## What I learned

Paid ads and conversion are one system. The ad decides who arrives and in what frame of mind; everything after decides whether they stay.

**Push traffic needs a reason to start. Pull traffic needs a reason to stop looking. Knowing which one you are paying for changes the ad, the page and the first question you ask.**
