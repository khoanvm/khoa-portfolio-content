At Clevermann I served as CEO from 2023 to 2026 with a practical focus on Marketing, Sales, partnerships and commercial growth. I helped build an organization of around seven people in Marketing, ten in Sales, and three in B2B Sales.

The work connected acquisition with screening, office visits, consultation, remarketing, nurturing and reactivation. Near-monthly internal hackathons and 86+ Marketing and Sales tests helped the team learn and act on bottlenecks. The business averaged around 20 contracts per month, with a peak of around 40.
