At a luxury real-estate developer, a satellite-testing approach helped us reduce cost per lead by approximately 30% within the campaigns being tested.

From a performance marketing perspective, that was an encouraging result.

But I didn't consider the number the complete answer.

A lower CPL is useful only when the inquiries remain relevant and the sales organization can respond effectively.

A prospect interested in a premium property is not necessarily ready to arrange a visit, discuss financing, or make a reservation.

The important questions continue after the lead enters the system.

Was the prospect reachable? Did the opportunity reach the right salesperson? Was the follow-up timely? Was there meaningful buying intent?

The initial testing showed no clear deterioration in lead quality, but the longer commercial journey still mattered.

**The lesson:** A metric can improve without telling us everything we need to know about business performance.

Better measurement begins by asking what happens next.
